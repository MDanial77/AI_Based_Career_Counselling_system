Features

More than a quiz. A counsellor that explains itself.

Every part of the system is designed around how students in Pakistan actually choose careers.

Education-aware matching

Recommends only careers you can pursue at your level. If none are open yet, you get a clear next-step pathway instead of a false match.

Understands your own words

Describe what you enjoy. Natural language processing picks out your interests and skills and compares them with every career.

Machine learning

Four classifiers are compared with 10-fold cross-validation; the best one adds a data-driven opinion to every recommendation.

Live results as you type

Your top matches update instantly while you fill the form, so you can see how marks, skills and interests change your options.

Skill-gap analysis

See which required skills you already have and which to learn next, with learning resources for each career.

Real jobs in Pakistan

Every recommended career links to current openings on Rozee.pk, LinkedIn and Indeed Pakistan, plus courses to get started.

FAQ

Common questions

Is AI Career Counselling free?

Yes. Create an account and take as many assessments as you like.

I am a Matric student. Is this useful for me?

Yes. Most careers need a degree, so instead of recommending jobs you cannot apply for yet, the system shows which careers match your interests and the qualification each one needs, so you can choose the right FSc or A Level group.

How does the AI decide?

Three techniques work together: knowledge-based scoring against each career's requirements, natural language processing of your description, and a machine learning model selected from four by 10-fold cross-validation. Every recommendation lists the reasons behind it. Read more.

Are the job listings real?

Yes. The job buttons open live searches on Rozee.pk, LinkedIn and Indeed Pakistan for that career, so you always see current openings.

What happens to my data?

Your profile and assessments are stored in this system's own database only to show your history and improve recommendations. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Should I rely only on these results?

No. Use the report as a starting point, and discuss important decisions with your teachers, parents or a career counsellor.
