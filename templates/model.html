How the AI works

Hybrid recommendation engine

Every recommendation combines three techniques:

1. Knowledge-based scoring

Matches your field, interest, education, academic results and skills against each career's requirements. Careers you are not yet eligible for become a next-step pathway.

2. Natural language processing

Your own description is tokenised, stemmed and compared with every career using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. Interests and skills mentioned in the text are detected automatically.

3. Machine learning

A classifier trained on labelled student profiles predicts the most likely careers for your profile. Its prediction contributes 20% of the final score.
