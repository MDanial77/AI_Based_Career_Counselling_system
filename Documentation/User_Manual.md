# User Manual: AI Career Counselling System

This manual is for students. It explains how to create an account, take the assessment, read your career report and look after your account.

## 1. Opening the website

Your teacher or the project team will start the system. Then open http://127.0.0.1:5000 in Chrome, Firefox or Edge. The site works on a computer, a tablet or a phone.

The home page explains what the system does, how it works and which career areas it covers. Click any career name under **Career areas** to see its details without signing in.

## 2. Creating an account

1. Click **Create account**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, age and gender.
3. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and type it again.
4. Choose a **recovery question** (for example "In which city were you born?") and write the answer. You will need it if you forget your password, so pick something you will remember.
5. Click **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.

## 3. Signing in and out

Click **Sign in**, enter your email and password, and click **Sign in**. To sign out, click your name at the top right and choose **Sign out**.

For your safety:
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is paused for 10 minutes.
- If you do nothing for 30 minutes, you are signed out automatically. Sign in again to continue.

## 4. Taking the assessment

The assessment has four parts. It takes about 10 minutes. The **live matches** panel shows your best careers while you fill in the form.

### Part 1: Education
- **Education level**: Matric, O Level, Intermediate, A Level, BS or MS.
- **Field of study**: the list changes with your level (for example Matric shows Biology, Computer Science and other groups).
- **Results**: your Matric percentage, your Intermediate percentage (if you have passed it), and for BS or MS your CGPA (0–4) or percentage.

### Part 2: Interest
- Choose the **main interest** that fits you best.
- Optionally, **describe in your own words** what you enjoy or would like to do, for example "I like helping patients and want to work in a hospital". The system reads this text (natural language processing) and uses it in the match.

### Part 3: Skills
- Tick up to **8 skills** you already have. Suggested skills for your interest appear first; use the search box or **Show all skills** for others.
- For every skill you tick, choose your **level**: 1 Beginner, 2 Basic, 3 Intermediate, 4 Advanced or 5 Expert. Be honest; a higher level counts for more.

### Part 4: Personality quiz
- Answer all **15 statements**, from **Strongly disagree** to **Strongly agree**. There are no right or wrong answers.
- The counter shows how many you have answered. You cannot submit until all 15 are answered.

Click **Get AI Career Recommendation**. Your report opens in a few seconds.

### Taking it again
When you come back, the form is already filled in with your last answers. Change only what is different (for example your new result after Intermediate) and submit again. Every report is saved separately.

## 5. Reading your career report

**Best match.** Your top career, its match percentage, salary, future scope and whether your education already qualifies.

**Your personality type.** Your two strongest Holland types (for example Social and Investigative) and a bar for each of the six types.

**Your roadmap.** Step-by-step from where you are now to your first job: Intermediate group, entry test, degree, licence, skills to build and the first job, with the years each step takes. Your next step is highlighted.

**All recommended careers.** Up to five careers. Each one shows:
- **Why this career**: the reasons, in plain language.
- **Personality match**: how well the career fits your quiz answers.
- **Skill match**: a bar and three groups of skills: green (you have it), amber (still developing, level 1 or 2) and grey (to learn).
- Salary, future scope and where to learn.
- **View roadmap** for that career, **View details**, **Find real jobs** (Rozee.pk, LinkedIn, Indeed Pakistan) and **Start learning** (Coursera, YouTube).

If no career is open at your level yet (for example after Matric), you see a **pathway** instead: what to study next to reach the careers that fit you.

**Feedback.** At the bottom, tell us whether the recommendations were helpful. This helps improve the system.

**Download PDF report.** Saves or prints the report.

## 6. Career details page

Click **View details** on any career to see its description, salary, future scope, minimum education, **required subjects** (Intermediate group, entry test, degree and licence), personality type, required skills, job and learning links and a full roadmap from Matric.

## 7. Your past reports

Click **My reports** at the top to see every assessment you have taken. Click one to open it exactly as it was.

## 8. Your profile and password

Click your name, then **Account settings**:
- Change your name, age or gender and click **Save details**.
- **Change password**: enter your current password and the new one twice, then click **Update password**.
- **Recovery question**: change your question and answer.

## 9. If you forget your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address.
3. Answer your recovery question (capital letters do not matter).
4. Choose a new password.

If you have also forgotten the answer, ask the administrator for a **reset link**. It works once and expires after 1 hour.

## 10. Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please answer all 15 personality questions" | Scroll to the quiz and answer the statements still without a choice; the counter shows how many are left |
| I cannot tick more skills | You can choose at most 8. Untick one first |
| The field of study I want is not in the list | Choose your education level first; the list depends on it |
| I was signed out | You were inactive for 30 minutes. Sign in again; your reports are saved |
| Sign-in is blocked | Wait 10 minutes after 5 wrong passwords, or use **Forgot password?** |

Recommendations are guidance, not a guarantee. Discuss important decisions with your teachers, parents or a career counsellor, and check admission rules with the university, because they change from year to year.
